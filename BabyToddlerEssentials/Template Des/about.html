The promise

Every choice, made with care

We test everything the way a parent would: pull it, chew it, wash it, drop it. If it wouldn't pass in our own homes, it doesn't make it to yours. That's the whole idea — treats you can hand over without a second thought, so the only thing left to do is watch them play.
